# BREAKBEAT 0.5 — Balance and depth

## Design goal

Novices survive through immediate single-group decisions. Skilled players get materially better results by synchronizing separate groups into a single pass, deciding whether an Overcharge's second-pass delay is safe, and catching physical midair contacts. No assist boosts, wildcard blocks, bombs or random power-ups were introduced.

## Pressure and board fairness

- Pressure rises on downbeats every **two bars**, tightening to **one bar**. Endless and Daily tighten at pass 22. Odyssey tightens at pass 24 for movement 1 and gradually earlier, to pass 16 for movement 6; Zen never pressures the player. Pressure is never coupled to the current score, so better players are not secretly penalized.
- The HUD shows the next push's exact bar countdown and wall headroom; on the preceding bar it previews the **actual incoming row**, generated by cloning—not advancing—the deterministic row RNG.
- Every new row contains at least one adjacent ordinary-color pair. No special blocks and no runtime network access are necessary.
- A pressure push that falls while a physics cascade is in flight remains deferred until a legal beat boundary; it does not interrupt the player's air-contact window.

## Scoring integrity

- Resonance for a crossing is based on **groups that actually detonate**, including prior detonations this same bar—not queued or held groups that may never clear.
- Resolve all simultaneous clears in stable order. Each tile earns points **at most once**, even when an Overcharge overlaps another primed group. A group wholly consumed by a neighbor's blast cannot also award a separate clear.
- The existing group size curve (`10 × size^1.35`), Resonance steps, unbroken-pass combo, ×1.25 air-prime, ×2 Overcharge, and score normalization remain. Mastery thresholds are not silently changed.
- A group detonating at a later sixteenth can increase the Resonance value for that later clear, but **earlier scores are never retrospectively modified**. This gives deterministic, immediately legible point feedback while avoiding speculative bonuses for possible future clears.

## Optional Endless skill contracts

Choose **STANDARD** or one of four opt-in contracts before entering Endless. Rules, generated tile set and core score math remain identical. Challenges are not enabled in Odyssey, Zen or Daily.

| Contract | Completion condition |
|---|---|
| Resonance Relay | Detonate four distinct groups in one measure |
| Overcharge Forge | Land three Overcharged blasts |
| Skyline Catch | Successfully air-prime three midfall groups |
| Pressure Gauntlet | Survive 12 actual pressure rows |

Completed contracts persist immediately in the device-local save, even if the player later quits. Challenge-mode results have separate local records, and no points are awarded for completing an objective. This is **not** a hosted/authoritative competitive ranking.

## Automated tuning evidence

Run `npm run balance:report` for a deterministic 40-seed comparison (42 bars maximum, BPM 116). Each named strategy is a *scripted policy*, not a human player:

- **Novice:** prime one small group every third bar; never hold or air-prime.
- **Intermediate:** prime up to two large groups every other bar; never hold or air-prime.
- **Expert:** prime up to four large groups every bar, strategically hold, and take visible air-prime windows.

| Scripted policy | Mean score | Mean bars reached | Mean groups cleared | Max groups in a bar (mean) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Novice | 137 | 17 | 5 | 1 |
| Intermediate | 2,985 | 27 | 23 | 2 |
| Expert | 11,290 | 42 | 94 | 5 |

The build gate repeats a 24-seed cohort and verifies a measurable novice→intermediate→expert gradient. This **demonstrates that game mechanics reward increasingly sophisticated inputs within the scripted scenarios; it does not validate real player retention, perceived fairness, or difficulty.** Those need real playtests before tuning is considered final.

## Validation and remaining risks

- `npm run gates` compiles TypeScript, runs the engine and balance tests, checks Pulse content isolation and purity, tests seeded board generation/preview, and validates the Vercel static output.
- Hardware FPS, audio-loopback calibration and complete end-to-end browser QA still require real-device testing. The CI simulation is deliberately not presented as a 60/120 FPS certification.
- Original multistem compositions are deferred until the dedicated final music pass. The current synthesized arrangements remain functional.
